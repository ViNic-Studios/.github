  - Problem with subtask modal. when creating a new task and then clicking create new subtask, you finish creating the subtask first but the parentID is not known yet so you can't assign the subtask directly to the parent.
  - adding existing task as subtask is a very specific list, no recurrences, no parents, no completed tasks... not necessarily all the inprogress + upcoming tasks.
